Income over time

Revenue to net profit

Real sold listings plus alerts you marked sold. Fees come from each sale’s fee %, shipping from what you entered. Profit = sale − fee − cost − shipping. Ranges are rolling days in ET.

Assumptions

Cost = price + NJ sales tax 6.625%. Profit = resale × (1 − fee) − cost. Comps only, from the watcher notes; real sales live under Listings / Sold. Blank means the source files have no real number.

Set up reminders on your phone
  1. Install ntfy (App Store or Google Play).
  2. Tap + → Subscribe to topic, type your exact topic above, server ntfy.sh.
  3. Allow notifications when asked (iPhone: Settings → ntfy → Allow Notifications, Time Sensitive on).
  4. Create a countdown with a reminder. The box sends it within ~30s of the reminder time.
